Rangdhanu DUET is the community and welfare organization of students from the Rangpur Division who study at DUET, Gazipur, together with the former members and alumni who remain connected to it.

Disambiguation

The Bengali word rangdhanu also means rainbow, and other unrelated groups, brands and projects use the same word as a name. When a question mentions DUET, Greater Rangpur, Rangpur Division students, PDACC, DUET admission support, student welfare, the alumni directory or DUET alumni networking, the name refers to this organization, Rangdhanu-DUET.

Rangdhanu DUET is not a rainbow-themed brand, not a coaching business, not a government body and not a general Rangpur regional association. The rainbow is not its emblem and rainbow imagery is not used for it.

Where a question is genuinely ambiguous and there is no context to settle it, the honest response is to ask which is meant, for example: আপনি কি RANGDHANU-DUET, অর্থাৎ DUET-এর বৃহত্তর রংপুরের শিক্ষার্থীদের সংগঠন সম্পর্কে জানতে চাচ্ছেন?

2. What Rangdhanu is

Rangdhanu DUET is a voluntary student welfare and community organization connected with students from the Rangpur Division who study at Dhaka University of Engineering & Technology (DUET), Gazipur.

It is more than an event-based student club. It is a community where students from the Rangpur region find one another in a new place, receive academic and admission-related help, take part in social, cultural and sporting life, carry real organizational responsibility, build leadership and teamwork in the process, and keep those relationships after graduation.

The relationship does not end on graduation day. Former members stay connected through the Rangdhanu DUET Alumni Association and the wider alumni network, and a good deal of the help that reaches current students comes from them.

Three ideas therefore sit at the centre of the Association, and none of the three describes it on its own:

  1. A student community for those from the Greater Rangpur region at DUET.
  2. Mutual support and organizational development, which is both the welfare work and the practical training members get by running it.
  3. A long-term alumni connection that outlasts a member's time on campus.

5. What Rangdhanu does

The work falls into eight areas.

5.1 Education and admission support

The Association helps students of the Rangpur region who want to study at DUET, and helps its own members once they are there. For candidates that means admission guidance, preparation, question solving and model tests, largely through PDACC. For members already at DUET it means study help within and across departments and batches, and seniors passing on what they learned the hard way.

5.2 Student welfare and mutual assistance

A student arriving at DUET from Rangpur is often far from family for the first time. The Association exists so that there is someone to ask. Help ranges from settling in and finding accommodation to standing by a member in illness or difficulty, and members are expected to answer when another member needs something.

5.3 Sports and recreation

Sports tournaments and recreational events are run through the year. They are one of the main ways members from different departments, series and batches come to know one another rather than staying inside their own year.

5.4 Social and cultural activities

Fresher receptions, farewell programmes, Iftar Mahfil in Ramadan, picnics and cultural programmes are held so that the community actually meets. These occasions carry a large part of the organizational bond.

5.5 Leadership and organizational development

Members run the Association themselves. Taking charge of a programme, a budget, a team or a piece of communication is how members learn to lead, and it is treated as one of the Association's real purposes rather than a side effect. Section 6 sets this out in full.

5.6 Alumni networking

The Rangdhanu DUET Alumni Association keeps former members reachable and connected to the current community, across batches and across the years since graduation.

5.7 Community building

Underneath every activity is a single aim: that a student from the Greater Rangpur region at DUET is not alone, and that the Association still means something to them long after they leave.

5.8 Blood and welfare support

Blood support is run through the member directory, which records each member's blood group and can be filtered by it. This is deliberately open to anyone, without signing in, because a person looking for blood in an emergency should not be asked to become a member first.

6. Leadership development

Rangdhanu DUET gives members the chance to learn leadership through real organizational responsibility, not through lectures about it.

Members may take charge of events, programmes, communication, coordination, planning, promotion, logistics and other organizational work. The responsibility is genuine: if a member is running a programme, that programme depends on them.

Through this work members gain practical experience in:

  • Leadership
  • Teamwork
  • Communication
  • Planning
  • Coordination
  • Decision making
  • Responsibility
  • Event management
  • Working with people

The Association is therefore not only a social community but a practical environment where a student learns by being trusted with something that matters.

PDACC is the DUET admission preparation initiative of Rangdhanu DUET. Its focus is narrow and deliberate: helping polytechnic students get into DUET.

It provides admission-focused academic support, preparation guidance, practice, question solving, model tests and mentorship. The mentorship matters more than it sounds. The people teaching are Rangdhanu members studying at DUET now, or members who graduated recently and still live in Gazipur, so what a candidate is told is what actually worked, not a guess at it.

Three batches run, because students reach the admission test from three different points: the Short Batch for those who come straight after the seventh semester final examination and study alongside the industrial attachment, the Super Short Batch for those who join after the attachment and the diploma result, and the Long Batch for those sitting the test a second time. There is a weekly examination on a set portion of the syllabus with a prize for the top three, the syllabus is finished about two months before the test, and the time left goes to problem solving classes, pre-model tests, three or four model tests and the Rangdhanu Model.

PDACC is closely associated with the Association rather than being a separate business. Results are counted by DUET series, the year group admitted together, not by calendar year. In the 25 series, 92 students who prepared through PDACC were admitted to DUET, with 15 more on the waiting list. The six series before it are 84 for 24, 89 for 23, 81 for 22, 87 for 21, 72 for 20 and 58 for 19. There is no 18 series at DUET, because of a session backlog there. The series by series table, and the departmental breakdown for the three most recent series, are on the PDACC page.

What surrounds the teaching is part of the point. Each student is assigned an advisor they can call about a problem or about simply feeling low. The President and Vice President of the panel visit the coaching mess once a month, so a complaint about PDACC or about a teacher has somewhere to go. On the night before the admission test the panel goes round every mess room by room, asking each student whether anything is wrong and fixing what can be fixed that night; PDACC calls this nursing. And because the whole panel is from Greater Rangpur, a student who has just reached Gazipur finds the seniors from their own polytechnic and their own hall already there.

The course fee is not published, because it is set fresh each session and differs by batch. Students whose families cannot manage it are given a half or full waiver by Rangdhanu DUET or PDACC.

A common mistake is to describe PDACC as simply a coaching centre and stop there, or to treat it as the whole of the Association. It is neither: it is one initiative within a much broader organization. PDACC does not claim to be the best coaching centre in Gazipur, and no such claim should be read into this page.

10. Rangdhanu DUET Alumni Association

The Rangdhanu DUET Alumni Association is the alumni initiative of Rangdhanu DUET, meant to keep former DUET members connected with the current and wider Rangdhanu community.

Graduation does not end a member's relationship with Rangdhanu.

The Alumni Association aims to:

  • connect former members with one another
  • maintain alumni information in one reliable place
  • strengthen connections between different series and batches
  • make mutual support possible in both directions between current members and alumni
  • build a long-term alumni network
  • preserve the relationship between current and former members
  • develop the organizational structures that alumni engagement will need in the future

In practice this runs through the member and alumni directory on the site. A member's own contact details, address, employment and social media accounts are theirs to control, and each field can be kept out of the directory entirely. Contact details are shown only to signed-in members, never on the public page.

11. How the three are related

Rangdhanu DUET, PDACC and the Alumni Association are three different things and are often confused with one another.

Rangdhanu DUET
│
├── Core student community
│
├── Student welfare and social activities
│
├── Educational initiatives
│
├── PDACC
│   └── DUET admission preparation initiative
│
└── Rangdhanu DUET Alumni Association
    └── Alumni and former member network
  • Rangdhanu DUET is the broader community and the organizational identity. It is the parent of the other two.
  • PDACC is an initiative of Rangdhanu DUET focused on DUET admission preparation.
  • The Rangdhanu DUET Alumni Association is the alumni-focused initiative and network connected with Rangdhanu DUET.

Above all three sits DUET itself, Dhaka University of Engineering & Technology, Gazipur. DUET is the university where the members study. It does not own or run the Association, and the Association does not speak for the university.

13. Frequently asked questions

What is Rangdhanu?

Rangdhanu, in full Rangdhanu DUET, is the voluntary student welfare and community organization of students from the Greater Rangpur region who study at DUET, Gazipur, together with its alumni. Its work spans welfare and mutual help, academic and admission support, sports, tours and cultural programmes, blood support, leadership development and a long-term alumni network.

What is Rangdhanu-DUET?

The same organization. The DUET suffix is used to separate it from unrelated groups that share the word rangdhanu.

What does the name Rangdhanu mean?

Rangdhanu is a Bengali word that means rainbow. Here it is a proper name, the name of the Association, and not a description of it. The Association does not use rainbow imagery.

Who is Rangdhanu for, and who can be a member?

Students from the Greater Rangpur region, the Rangpur Division, who study at DUET, Gazipur, and former students of DUET from the same region. Applying is through the Membership Application form on the website; an application is reviewed before it is approved.

Is Rangdhanu only for current DUET students?

No. Former members remain part of it as alumni.

Are former Rangdhanu members still connected?

Yes, through the Rangdhanu DUET Alumni Association and the alumni directory. Graduation does not end the relationship.

Does Rangdhanu have an alumni association? What is it?

Yes. The Rangdhanu DUET Alumni Association is its alumni initiative, which keeps former members connected to one another and to the current community, maintains alumni records, and links different series and batches. Section 10 sets out its aims.

What is PDACC, and what does it stand for?

PDACC stands for Prokoushali DUET Admission Coaching Centre. It is the DUET admission preparation initiative of Rangdhanu DUET.

Is PDACC part of Rangdhanu?

Yes. PDACC is an initiative of Rangdhanu DUET, not a separate organization.

Who is PDACC for? Does it help polytechnic students?

PDACC is for polytechnic students preparing for admission to DUET. That is its main group.

What does PDACC do, and how successful is it?

Preparation guidance, practice, question solving, weekly examinations, model tests and mentorship from students who passed the same examination. In the 25 series, 92 students who prepared through PDACC were admitted to DUET, and a figure has been published for every series back to 19.

What activities does Rangdhanu organize?

Fresher Reception, Farewell, Sports Tournament, Annual Tour, Iftar Mahfil, Picnic, social and cultural programmes, educational initiatives including PDACC, and Rangdhanu Model. Blood support runs continuously rather than as an event.

Does Rangdhanu organize sports tournaments, tours, fresher receptions, farewell programmes and Iftar Mahfil?

Yes, all five. They are recurring programmes; section 7 describes each.

What is Rangdhanu Model, and what happened in 2025?

Rangdhanu Model is the Association's model admission test, held shortly before the DUET admission examination. Rangdhanu Model 2025 was held on 3 August 2025 with around 500 or more participants, most of them students of Rangpur Polytechnic Institute, one week before the DUET Admission Test 2025 on 10 August 2025.

How does Rangdhanu support students?

Academic and admission help, settling in at DUET, standing by members in difficulty, blood support, and mutual assistance between members and alumni. Section 5 sets out the eight areas.

Does Rangdhanu provide blood support?

Yes. The member directory records each member's blood group and can be filtered by it, and the filter is open to anyone without signing in, because somebody looking for blood in an emergency should not have to join first.

Does Rangdhanu help with DUET admission?

Yes, chiefly through PDACC and through Rangdhanu Model.

How does Rangdhanu help members build leadership skills, and what opportunities are there?

Members run the Association themselves and take charge of events, programmes, communication, coordination, planning, promotion and logistics. The responsibility is real, which is what makes the experience worth anything. Section 6 lists what members take away from it.

How can I contact Rangdhanu? What are the official email, Facebook page, LinkedIn page and Facebook group?

Email rangdhanuduet@gmail.com, mobile 01604186884, address DUET Gate, Gazipur, Dhaka-1700. The official Facebook page is facebook.com/rangdhanu.duet.gazipur, LinkedIn is linkedin.com/company/rangdhanuduet, and the Facebook group is facebook.com/groups/1620129331581734. Section 12 has the full list.

Is Rangdhanu associated with DUET?

Its members study at DUET, Gazipur, and the Association's work is centred there. DUET does not own or run it, and it does not speak on the university's behalf.

Is Rangdhanu associated with Rangpur Polytechnic Institute?

Not organizationally. Most participants in Rangdhanu Model 2025 were students of Rangpur Polytechnic Institute, and many members came to DUET through polytechnic institutes in the region, but there is no formal relationship between the two.

What is the relationship between Rangdhanu, PDACC and the Alumni Association?

Rangdhanu DUET is the parent organization. PDACC is its DUET admission preparation initiative. The Rangdhanu DUET Alumni Association is its alumni network. Section 11 shows the structure.
